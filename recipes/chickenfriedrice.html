Chicken Fried Rice Recipe

Recipe Description

This easy chicken fried rice recipe tastes just like your favorite hibachi restaurant!

On the table in just about 20 minutes, this yummy dish is perfect as a main course or as a side, and is guaranteed to please the whole family!

Ingredients

  • Salt
  • Pepper
  • Garlic
  • Rice
  • Chicken
  • Soy Sauce
  • Various Vegetables
  • Eggs

How to: All the steps

  1. Cook rice: until is good for your taste!
  2. Prepare meat: Season the meat as you like and set it aside!
  3. Combine all the ingredients: Stir the mixture together in a Wok.
  4. Let it rest and serve: After a few minutes, it's ready to serve!! Enjoy the deliciousness!
